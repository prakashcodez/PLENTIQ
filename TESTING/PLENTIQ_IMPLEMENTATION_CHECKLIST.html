Email validation is not email verification

We can validate email format, prevent duplicate addresses, require a password of at least 8 characters containing a letter, number and symbol, and store a hash without emailing an OTP. That does not prove the mailbox belongs to the person. Do not label an account “email verified” unless a real verification flow exists.
